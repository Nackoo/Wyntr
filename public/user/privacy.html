Wyntr Privacy policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Wyntr (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal data through https://wyntr.netlify.app ("Wyntr").

information we collect

we collect several informations you yourself provided inside the platform. including:

  • your username
  • your display name
  • your banner image
  • your avatar image
  • your description

and we collect several informations you provided during authorization. including:

  • your google email address
  • your sign-in date
  • your user id

how we use your data

  • To authenticate you via google authorization
  • To provide, maintain, and improve Wyntr platform

data sharing

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data
  • We share limited information with:
    • firebase firestore for database infrastructure
    • firebase auth for authentication

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account exists or as needed to provide and maintain Wyntr. If you decide to stop using Wyntr, you can request that your account and all associated data be permanently deleted (excluding your replies to another post that's not yours). To request data deletion, please contact us through our Discord Server

Security

We rely on Firebase's built-in security and encryption to store and manage your data securely. However, no online service is 100% secure, so please use Wyntr responsibly.

Children’s Privacy

Our service is for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

International Data

Wyntr operates in United States of America. Your data may be processed outside your country of residence.

Changes to policy

We may update this policy periodically. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions, contact us at our Discord Server
